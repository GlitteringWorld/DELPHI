# DELPHI
A Sonic Spatial Composer

Delphine is an augmented reality experience that turns the room
around you into a musical instrument. A canopy of stars forms
overhead, and Delphinus, the dolphin constellation, comes to life to
tell how a musician’s lyre drew it from the sea. As the sky fades, the
dolphin invites you to make music of your own. You pinch pieces of
stardust, move them to change their pitch and instrument, then place
them around the room and play them back to create a composition in
space.

Delphine brings us back to a more instinctive way of making music:
with our bodies and our ears. Instead of starting with a written score
or thinking about note names, keys, and time signatures, you move,
listen, and follow what sounds right to you. The experience sets aside
the layers of notation and theory that Western music has accumulated
over centuries so anyone can compose. You don’t have to know what
to call a sound to know what you want to hear next.
